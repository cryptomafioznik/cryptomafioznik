# Productized services

Two fixed-price packages. Payment in USDC/USDT through `checkout/` (products `app-in-7-days` and `aso-audit`) or via Upwork for clients who need an invoice from a platform.

## Package 1: iOS app from idea to App Store in 7 days — $1,500

What the client gets:
- Scope call (30 min) and a one-page spec on day 1.
- Native SwiftUI app, up to 5 screens, local data, no backend. Backend or subscriptions: +$500 and +5 days.
- App Store listing: name, subtitle, keywords, description, 5 screenshots, privacy policy page.
- Submission under the client's developer account, one round of review fixes.
- Source code in the client's GitHub.

Not included: design from scratch beyond a clean system-style UI, Android, ongoing maintenance (offered at $300/month for updates and store replies).

Proof: 5 shipped App Store apps (construction tools, rental inspection, photo cleaner), listed by name on the profile.

## Package 2: App Store page audit and localization — $150

- Written audit of one listing: keyword coverage vs. the top 12 for 20 queries, screenshot story, pricing structure, review prompt timing.
- Rewritten title, subtitle, keyword field, promo text and description in English.
- Metadata localized into 5 languages (es, de, fr, pt-BR, ru or client's pick).
- Delivered in 5 business days as a document the client pastes into App Store Connect.

Upsell: implement it plus a second round after 30 days of data, $150.

## Where to list

- Upwork: profile title "iOS developer: I ship App Store apps in 7 days". Both packages as Project Catalog items (Upwork allows fixed-scope projects). Payout: Payoneer or direct to bank; W-8BEN form only.
- Fiverr: same two gigs, "App Store page audit" tends to sell first because the price is low and the deliverable is clear.
- Reddit r/iOSProgramming, r/SideProject, r/indiehackers: never post the offer. Answer "how do I get downloads" and "how long does an app take" threads with specifics; mention the audit only when someone asks for help with their listing.
- Threads/X: build-in-public posts with real numbers from the construction apps; the offer sits in the bio.

## Intake form (for `aso-audit` delivery link)

A simple form (Tally or Google Forms, free): App Store link, target countries, current monthly downloads (optional), main competitor, what they tried already, email. The checkout's `delivery.url` for `aso-audit` points here.
