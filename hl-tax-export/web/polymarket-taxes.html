Polymarket taxes: exporting your trades

Updated October 2026.

The problem

Polymarket shows your positions and activity in the app, but there is no CSV export and no annual statement. Every trade is a swap on Polygon between USDC and an outcome token (for example "Will X happen? / Yes"), and every redemption of a winning share is a swap back to USDC. For tax purposes each of those is a disposal of a crypto asset in most countries, and a tax tool that imports your Polygon wallet sees hundreds of unnamed ERC-1155 token movements.

What the exporter does

Enter your Polymarket proxy wallet (the address shown on your profile, not the wallet you log in with) in the exporter. It reads the public data API and writes every trade as a swap:

TradeKoinly row
Buy 100 Yes shares at 0.40Sent 40 USDC, received 100 PM:Will-X-happen/Yes
Sell 100 Yes shares at 0.90Sent 100 PM:Will-X-happen/Yes, received 90 USDC

The outcome token keeps the market title so you and your accountant can read the ledger. Koinly treats each PM: symbol as a custom asset and computes the gain on the sell.

Redemptions and deposits

When a market resolves, winning shares are redeemed on-chain for 1 USDC each. That happens on Polygon, outside the trades API. Import your Polygon wallet alongside the Polymarket file: the redemption appears there as outgoing shares and incoming USDC, which closes the position. Deposits from an exchange and withdrawals also live on the Polygon wallet.

Finding your proxy wallet

  1. Open your Polymarket profile.
  2. Copy the 0x address shown under your name. That is the proxy wallet that holds positions.
  3. Paste it into the exporter's Polymarket field. The Hyperliquid field can stay empty.

Fees

Most Polymarket markets charge no trading fee as of 2026. Where a market has fees, they reduce the USDC received and show up in the price, so no separate fee row is needed.

US users

Polymarket's regulated US launch in 2026 did not add a tax export at the time of writing. Trades remain property disposals; keep the per-trade ledger regardless of whether a form arrives later.
