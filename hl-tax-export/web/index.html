Questions


Does Koinly support Hyperliquid?


Koinly can sync a Hyperliquid address, but its own forum has threads about unmatched purchase history, perp closes treated as disposals of assets you never held, and staked HYPE not showing. A clean universal CSV avoids all three: every perp close is a realized gain or loss, fees are costs, funding is summed per day.


How is funding taxed?


Funding settles every hour while a perp position is open, up to 8,760 times per position per year. Most tools and accountants treat the net amount as part of trading profit and loss. Pick the label that matches your accountant's method.


Is my data sent anywhere?


No. Your browser calls the public Hyperliquid and Polymarket APIs and builds the files locally. Only a license key, if you enter one, is verified against our server.


What about Polymarket redemptions?


Winning shares are redeemed on-chain on Polygon and are not in the trades API. Import your Polygon wallet alongside this file; the outcome tokens then resolve to USDC.


Nothing on this page is tax advice. Tax treatment of perpetuals and prediction markets differs by country.
